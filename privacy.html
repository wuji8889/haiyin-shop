Privacy Policy — Haiyin Health （海音保健）

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Haiyin Health （海音保健） is a health products store that accepts Pi payments. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to process an order:

  • Name — the name you provide at checkout.
  • Phone number — the phone number you provide at checkout, so we can contact you about your order.
  • Shipping address — the address you provide at checkout, so we can deliver your order.

We do not ask for any other personal information. No account is required to shop in our store, and we do not ask you to register or log in.

2. How Orders Are Stored

Your order is stored locally in your own browser (using localStorage on your device). We do not keep a copy of your order on our servers except as described below.

When you pay, the order details are sent to the store backend only to process the Pi payment. The backend uses them solely to verify and complete that payment.

3. Pi Payments

Payments are processed through the Pi Network platform. When you pay with Pi, your payment is handled by Pi Network under its own terms and privacy rules. We do not receive or store your Pi wallet password, passphrase, or private key — never share those with anyone, including us.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • process your order and arrange delivery;
  • contact you about your order if needed;
  • handle returns or refunds.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We share your information only when necessary to complete your order — for example, with the delivery service that ships your package — and only the information needed for that purpose.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Testnet Notice

The store currently runs on Pi Testnet. Orders placed now are test sales only, and no real shipping takes place. Any information provided during this test phase is used only for testing the store and the payment flow.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date shown above.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ymjl8899@gmail.com.
